
# Scrum 

From my work experience I have this knowledge around Scrum,
when thinking about how to set time around tasks.
---

It is really important to have a structure like 

- Epic
- Feature
- Story
- Task

And organize it well, breaking it down to small tasks.

When dealing with something smaller, like a task for updating a component to behave a bit different it is easier to correctly set a realistic time on the workitem.

For bigger tasks, if it is for more than one person and longer than a sprint, the whole team gets involved and sits down together. 

We tried Planning Poker where we kinda quickly went through all tasks and everyone had do guess an estimate. If everyone were close to each other that time got picked and if someone stood out they got a quick word in explaining why they thought differently.

This worked but we went back to just looking at the tasks together, picking what we wanted, and said our time. 

It is better to get it done and take on more, than to estimate wrong, ending up being behind.
